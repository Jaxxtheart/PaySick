How the rights transfer works

A licence is not a receipt for a file. It is an instrument, and it is recorded as one.

01

The asset is locked

Before anything is priced, the asset row is locked. Two buyers cannot be told they both hold exclusivity over the same footage.

02

Conflicts are checked

Live grants, plan entitlement, property and model releases, and conservation controls are all evaluated before a cent moves.

03

Sale and transfer settle together

The licence, the payment, the revenue split and the rights transfer are written in one transaction. If any part fails, none of it happened.

04

The chain of title is appended

Each transfer adds a hash-linked entry to the asset's chain of title, and you get a rights certificate carrying that hash. Provenance stays verifiable.

What the licence carries

Scope, territory and duration are priced separately because they are separate rights. A personal print, an editorial still, a brand campaign and a worldwide broadcast are not the same grant, and exclusivity is only available on the Broadcast & Rights tier. A full buyout assigns the copyright outright — the asset leaves the catalogue permanently and the chain of title records the new holder.

Two rules are conservation policy rather than commerce. Commercial use of imagery captured on SANParks land requires a signed property release. And no asset featuring a species at poaching risk is ever released with its location metadata attached, at any licence scope, to anyone — a geotagged rhino is a map.
