# Requirements & Specifications — PaySick v1.11.1

**Version**: 1.11.1
**Date**: 2026-09-29

Carries forward all requirements from v1.11.0 and its predecessors, and adds:

## Investor Deck (`investor-deck.html`)

- Slide counters run `01 / 17` to `17 / 17` in order with no duplicates.
- No claim may contradict another slide or the shipped platform: no 60-second
  approval claim; provider payout copy matches the 80/20 two-stage payout.
- Data integrations that are not live must be labelled as roadmap and must
  not name partners without an agreement in place.
- The ask slide shows the $250,000 request with dollar use of funds and the
  $8M seed as context; no valuation/equity terms until the funding type is
  confirmed.
- The HTML slides and the client-side PPTX generator must carry the same figures.

## Deprecated Features

None.
